# QA summary

QA means quality assurance: checking a department's actual output against evidence and agreed acceptance criteria before it progresses. This page summarises the rules; it is not a QA approval for any episode.

## How each task progresses

1. The department researches and completes its authorised deliverable.
2. The author self-checks and fixes obvious defects.
3. A separate human or separate AI reviewer checks the actual version, records evidence and issues PASS or FAIL.
4. Failed work returns to its department for correction and re-review. If a reviewer is unavailable, use REVIEWER NEEDED; self-check alone is not independent PASS.
5. Cristian accepts or requests changes to the reviewed version and explicitly authorises the named next task.
6. The team records the decision and provides a ready-to-forward department prompt. The next department verifies inputs and approval before starting.

Cristian may act as both independent reviewer and executive approver if both decisions are explicit. No average quality score can cancel a critical defect.

## What reviewers check

| Department | Core checks |
|---|---|
| Editorial / Research | Correct premise, trustworthy sources, traceable claims, counterevidence and uncertainty |
| Story / Script | Factual accuracy, clear narrative, useful payoff, no padding, five-second maximum hook |
| Art / Design | Consistent original characters, accurate context, readable labels, asset rights |
| Voice / Audio | Correct pronunciation, stable voice, complete narration, intelligibility and rights |
| Storyboard / Editing | Script-image agreement, timing, continuity, captions, complete export and full watch-through |
| Packaging / Publishing | Honest title/thumbnail, correct metadata/settings, exact release approval |
| Automation | Real capability tests, budget controls, safe retries, approval checks and recovery |
| Analytics | Reliable data, appropriate comparisons and clearly labelled hypotheses |

## Non-negotiable blockers

Unsupported central claims, misleading visuals, missing rights, broken exports or missing approvals block progression/release. Changes to approved content reopen affected gates. Publication always needs separate explicit approval of the exact release package.

## Status language

- NOT RUN: no review completed.
- SELF-CHECK ONLY: reviewed by its own author.
- REVIEWER NEEDED: independent review outstanding.
- FAIL: identified criteria not met.
- PASS: independent checks completed with evidence; still requires Cristian's acceptance.

Every submission includes a short QA summary plus a draft next-department prompt. Failed tasks get a correction prompt; missing-reviewer tasks get a review prompt. See templates/department_handoff.md and SOP Section 19.
